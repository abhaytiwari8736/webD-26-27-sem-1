iPhone 18 Pro Max

The iPhone 18 Pro Max is Apple's 2026 flagship smartphone, featuring a 6.9-inch 120Hz OLED display, the advanced 2nm A20 Pro chip for next-gen Apple Intelligence, and a triple 48MP camera system with variable aperture. It delivers exceptional battery life up to 43 hours and is built with a rugged, premium aluminum unibody.

Features
  • Performance & AI

    • A20 Pro Chip: Built on a 2-nanometer process with a 6-core CPU and 7-core GPU for console-level gaming. Dual Neural Engine: Dual 16-core processors dedicated to running advanced, on-device Apple Intelligence and real-time Siri tasks.

  • Camera System

    • Variable Aperture: The 48MP Pro Fusion main camera mechanically adjusts its aperture to optimize light capture in any condition. Triple 48MP Array: Features a 48MP Main, 48MP Ultra Wide, and 48MP Telephoto lens for high-resolution zooming. 18MP Front Camera: Upgraded Center Stage selfie camera with advanced spatial video capabilities.

  • Display & Design

    • 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR: Large OLED screen with 120Hz ProMotion and a peak outdoor brightness of 3,000 nits. Aluminum Unibody: Sleek, durable design protected by next-generation Ceramic Shield glass on the front and back. Physical Controls: Includes both the customizable Action Button and the tactile Camera Control interface.

  • Battery & Charging

    • 43-Hour Battery Life: High-capacity 5,567 mAh battery optimized for long endurance. Ultra-Fast Charging: Recharges up to 50% battery in approximately 15 minutes.
